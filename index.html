Good to know

Questions, answered.

Here are a few things people ask before booking their first thermal inspection.

What can a thermal inspection tell me?

Thermal cameras show surface temperature differences. Those patterns can point to concerns such as moisture, air leakage, insulation gaps, or overheating electrical components. Follow-up investigation may be needed to confirm the cause.

Is the inspection invasive?

No. The camera reads infrared energy from surfaces; it does not see through walls or require us to open them. We scan accessible areas and explain any limitations in your report.

How long does an inspection take?

Most residential visits take around 60–90 minutes, depending on property size and the areas you’d like us to check. We’ll confirm timing when we learn more about your project.

When will I get my report?

We aim to deliver a clear, annotated report within 24 hours after the site visit.

Can thermal imaging find every leak or defect?

No inspection method can find every issue under all conditions. Results depend on temperature differences, access, weather, and other site conditions. We explain what the images show and recommend further evaluation where appropriate.
